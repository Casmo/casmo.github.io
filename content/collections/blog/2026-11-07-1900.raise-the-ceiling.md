The way we build apps might be changing, but the fundamentals are not. In fact. It is more important that ever.
Whoever has been around internet long enough recognize all the "trends" that have been. From layouts in tables, iframes, flaming text GIFs, flat design, parallax scrolling and the bright AI colors. They all come and go and have been adopted one way or another.
However, since anything seems a prompt away, that typical AI style popups everywhere. From posts of local sports clubs and colouring pages on schools. Whoever works with AI can immediately see it is AI. You may even see what model is used. And to be fair; if you are not a designer, it looks good pretty quickly. "You made this!"

I do think this is the same with building apps (or games). In a very short time you can make something that looks pretty amazing. And, as someone who has been building for 30 years, I also feel (sometimes) that "I can build something amazing" in a short period of time.
But is it? Building something that looks good is completely different than something that is good. And here we need to be realistic. Building something good still require a solid foundation, time and effort and (a bit of) human touch. We need to raise the ceiling too. Drastically!
